Turbo Oil Corporation

Integrated oil and gas solutions across upstream, midstream and downstream operations.

01 / Company overview

Energy for a
world in motion.

Turbo Oil Corporation is a forward-thinking energy company specializing in the exploration, production and distribution of oil and gas worldwide.

Headquartered in Dubai, the company operates across Thailand, Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos, Hong Kong and Singapore, delivering integrated upstream, midstream and downstream solutions.

Committed to technical innovation, operational excellence and sustainability, Turbo Oil meets the evolving energy demands of the global economy. With over five years of experience, advanced technology and strong infrastructure support reliable, efficient and sustainable energy solutions that adhere to global industry standards.

05 / Turbo Oil Premium Lubricants

Protection that
goes further.

Turbo Oil offers a full range of API and SAE standard lubricants. The Premium Lubricant line delivers superior protection and performance across automotive, industrial and specialized applications.

  • PCMO Passenger vehicles
  • MCO 2-stroke & 4-stroke motorcycles
  • HDDEO Heavy-duty diesel engines
  • Hydraulic & gear oils Heavy-duty systems
  • Marine engine oils Maritime & river transport
  • Agricultural oils Tractors & farm equipment
