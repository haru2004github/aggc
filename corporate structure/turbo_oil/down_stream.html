Refining, distribution & trading

Turbo Oil’s downstream activities cover refining, distribution, marketing and trading. Its portfolio includes global petroleum and petrochemical trade, LNG / LPG and fuel retail.

Products & services

  • Petroleum and petrochemical trade
  • LNG / LPG
  • Fuel retail

Premium lubricants

API and SAE standard lubricants serve passenger vehicles, motorcycles, heavy-duty engines and specialized industrial applications.

  • PCMO: passenger vehicle engine oils
  • MCO: oils for 2-stroke and 4-stroke motorcycles
  • HDDEO: heavy-duty diesel engine oils
  • Hydraulic and gear oils for heavy-duty systems
  • Marine engine oils for maritime and river transport
  • Agricultural oils for tractors and farm equipment
  • Multi-purpose, high-temperature industrial greases
